# UX/UI suggestions (not built)

These are proposals to make ProspectAI more engaging and easier to navigate. None of them are in the app yet; the current layout (map in the middle, features behind icon rails) stays as it is. Each one adds to the existing design rather than replacing it.

## Navigation

1. **Command palette (⌘K / long-press the logo).** One search box that jumps to any feature, keyword, business or setting: "add keyword", "Kreyol Bistro", "turn on TV ads". This is the fastest way to move around once there are many features.
2. **First-run tour in 4 steps.** Highlight one icon at a time: add a keyword → move the fence → watch a signal blink → open a package. Skippable, and shown once.
3. **Labels on long-press (phones).** The bottom dock is icon-only; holding an icon for half a second could show its name, the same way the desktop tooltips do.
4. **Breadcrumb inside drawers.** When a package opens from the map, show "Map › Kreyol Bistro" so people know how to get back.

## Engagement

5. **"Moment" cards on the map.** When a package is sent or a call is booked, float a small card from the business pin for 3 seconds ("Email + YouTube ad sent to Kreyol Bistro"). This shows the agent working without opening a drawer.
6. **Daily goal ring.** A ring around the Pipeline MRR tile that fills toward a target (for example 3 booked calls a day), with a short celebration when it closes.
7. **Heatmap toggle.** Switch the map from live blinks to a 7-day heatmap of keyword demand, to show where to move the fence.
8. **Time scrubber.** A slider under the map to replay the last hour or day of signals, useful for demos and for explaining results to clients.
9. **Sound and haptics (opt-in).** A soft tick for in-fence signals and a chime for booked calls, plus a short vibration on phones.

## Clarity

10. **Package timeline view.** In a package, show the channels as a horizontal timeline (Day 1 email → Day 5 YouTube → Day 9 TV) with each step's status, instead of chips only.
11. **Empty-state coaching.** When nothing is happening, suggest a next step: "No signals in 10 minutes. Widen the fence to 2.5 mi or add 'catering near me'."
12. **Confidence badges on voice matches.** Show the match score next to voice keywords (already computed) and let people approve low-confidence matches before a package is sent.
13. **Client-facing share view.** A read-only link per business showing their demand map and competitor report, which turns the report into the sales pitch.

## Accessibility

14. A **high-contrast mode** and a **text-size control** in Connection & API settings.
15. **Keyboard shortcuts** (1–5 left drawers, 6–0 right drawers, Esc to close), with a "?" overlay listing them.
